Practice & research

Questions that
move the work.

My research brings sensory experience and audience participation into dialogue with choreographic practice.

I.

The senses & immersive narrative

Exploring how the five senses and concepts from neuroscience can inform storytelling in immersive dance theatre. This project brings questions of human nature, morality and love into movement and spatial relationships.

Sensory experience · Movement-based narrative · Spatial storytelling

II.

The audience & embodied experience

An investigation into the theory and practice of immersive dance theatre, including choreographic approaches, creative processes and research methods. The work considers how movement, spatial interaction and participation can engage audiences with cultural and social questions.

Immersive dance theatre · Audience engagement · Creative methodologies

III.

Place, history & cultural memory

Research into the integration of Jiangxi’s revolutionary heritage and local culture into immersive dance theatre. A script centred on the Nanchang Uprising explores the relationship between place, historical narrative and audience experience.

Script development · Jiangxi’s local culture · Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis
